Don't Unfold — Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Data we collect

None. Don't Unfold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. The developer receives no information about you.

Photos

With your permission, the app reads the home screen screenshots you take or choose, to show them as a fake home screen. They are copied into the app's own storage on this phone and never uploaded or shared. Text recognition that finds the clock in the screenshot runs on the device.

Deleting

Tap the trash button next to a screenshot, or delete the app, to remove everything it stored.
